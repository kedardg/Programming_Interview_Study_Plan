The Interactive Notebook

Twenty-seven chapters that replace passive reading with animations you can step through, calculators you can argue with, and flashcards that make it stick. The arc runs from the math under everything, through deep learning and transformers, into GPUs, kernels, and the systems that train and serve LLMs — the same ground as the study plan, in notebook form. Every page works offline; open this folder with any static server (python3 -m http.server) or straight from disk.

The learning path behind this notebook

The notebook follows the study plan in this repo and the learning path distilled from Alisa Liu's Notes on the Industry Job Search (57 interviews across 11 companies after an NLP PhD), with her Book of LLMs and math notes, Nathan Lambert's job-hunt data, Stanford CS336, and How to Scale Your Model as the spine (mirrored in the companion job-prep repo). How to use this with the plan

  • Weeks 1–4: chapters 1–3 and 8 alongside PMPP, plus Coding Patterns (18) for the Monday/Wednesday problems; decks Math, Deep Learning, GPU, Coding Patterns.
  • Weeks 5–8: chapters 4–7, 9, 10 while you build the kernels; drill Kernels + Quantization decks before each Saturday build.
  • Weeks 9–11: chapters 11–18; inference and parallelism are the mental model for the vLLM exercise, Customization/RL Systems/Frameworks (14–16) are the model-customization track, and RL & Robotics (17) pairs with the Sunday robot-learning slots.
  • Weeks 12–13: chapters 19–21 plus the Notes Library and the System Design deck — then drill all due cards daily in interview mode.
