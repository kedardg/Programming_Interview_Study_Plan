Why three models, when every other chapter in this notebook works on one? Because the three are shaped differently enough that the same technique lands differently on each, and the differences are exactly the things an engineer gets asked about in a customization engagement. G P T O S S twenty B is a mixture of experts whose expert weights ship in M X F P four, a four bit block floating point format, with a chat format called harmony that has channels for reasoning and tool calls. Qwen three four B is a small dense model with a thinking switch, and it is the one model on which you can run every technique in this notebook on a sixteen gigabyte card. Qwen three point eight twenty seven B is a dense hybrid, where forty eight of its sixty four layers are gated delta net layers that keep a fixed size state instead of a growing K V cache, plus a multi token prediction head and native vision. If you can explain what changes when you fine tune and deploy each of these, you can do it for any model a customer brings.

Start with the one glance comparison, because it decides everything downstream. G P T O S S twenty B has about twenty one billion total parameters but only about three point six billion active per token, since each token is routed to four of thirty two experts in every layer. On disk it is roughly thirteen gigabytes, because the experts are already four bit. Attention alternates between banded layers with a window of one hundred twenty eight tokens and full global layers, with learned attention sinks, and the context is one hundred twenty eight thousand tokens. Qwen three four B is four billion dense parameters, thirty six layers with grouped query attention and Q K norm, eight gigabytes in sixteen bit, thirty two thousand tokens native and over one hundred thousand with yarn scaling. Its K V cache is the largest of the three per token, about one hundred forty four kibibytes, because it has full attention in every layer. Qwen three point eight twenty seven B is twenty seven billion dense parameters, fifty four gigabytes in sixteen bit, a two hundred sixty two thousand token context, and because only sixteen of its layers hold a K V cache it needs only about sixty four kibibytes per token. Memory per token, weight bytes, and the attention pattern are the three numbers that drive every decision that follows.

The first interactive is the fit calculator. Pick a model, a technique, a G P U, a sequence length and a micro batch, and it stacks the memory the way the training chapter taught: weights, gradients, optimizer state, activations, and for G R P O the rollout engine and its K V cache. The numbers to remember are that a full fine tune in sixteen bit with Adam costs sixteen bytes per parameter, and that activations cost about thirty four times the hidden size per token per layer, or about two times the hidden size with gradient checkpointing. Play with it and three facts fall out. Qwen three four B fits a full fine tune on one eighty gigabyte card and a Q lora run on a sixteen gigabyte card with room to spare. G P T O S S twenty B fits attention only lora on sixteen gigabytes because its experts stay frozen in M X F P four, but a full fine tune needs the experts dequantized to sixteen bit first, which is over forty gigabytes of weights alone and about three hundred forty gigabytes all in, so it is an eight H one hundred job. Qwen three point eight twenty seven B is a rented H one hundred for anything beyond short sequence Q lora.

Now the playbooks, and each one has a distinct move. The G P T O S S playbook starts with format, because harmony is the gotcha everyone hits. There are roles for system, developer, user, assistant and tool, and three channels: analysis for the chain of thought, commentary for tool calls, and final for the answer the user sees. Reasoning effort, low, medium or high, is set in the system message. Training data has to be rendered in harmony with the analysis channel either kept or dropped consistently, and the loss masked to the assistant turns. The distinct fine tuning move is choosing where lora attaches. Lora on the attention projections is cheap and leaves the four bit experts untouched. Lora on the expert tensors is possible through the target parameters feature in P E F T, but it requires dequantizing the experts, so it is a forty gigabyte job and a different class of machine. For serving, the experts stay M X F P four, F P eight applies only to the dense parts, and the K V cache can go to F P eight. The serving command needs the open A I G P T O S S reasoning parser and the open A I tool call parser so that channels become structured reasoning and tool call fields. Hopper and Blackwell cards get the fast attention path with sinks through flash attention three; on older cards the experts are dequantized to sixteen bit and the attention falls back, which is why a sixteen gigabyte consumer card serves it but slowly.

The Qwen three four B playbook is the full ladder on one card. Format first: the hybrid thinking switch means your training data must be consistent about whether the think block is present, and empty think tags are the convention for non thinking examples. Then supervised fine tuning, where everything fits. Q lora at two thousand tokens uses under a third of a sixteen gigabyte card, lora in sixteen bit fits with headroom, and a full fine tune fits one eighty gigabyte G P U. Preference tuning with D P O works with a lora policy and the base as the reference by switching the adapters off. The distinct move is that this is the model to learn reinforcement learning on. G R P O with verifiable rewards, four or eight samples per prompt, a colocated V L L M rollout engine on the same card at a thirty five percent memory budget, and five hundred twelve completion tokens, is a real run on a sixteen gigabyte card. After training, merge the adapter, quantize to A W Q four bit weights or F P eight with L L M compressor, serve with the Qwen three reasoning parser and the hermes tool parser, and load test both thinking modes separately, because thinking tokens change the cost per request by a large factor. Deploy it on L four or L forty S instances and scale with replicas, not tensor parallelism.

The Qwen three point eight twenty seven B playbook is about what changes when most of the layers are not attention. Format gets vision and a thinking switch and the multi token prediction head. For fine tuning, lora on all linear layers still works, but the gated delta net projections have their own module names, so check the target modules, and training needs the flash linear attention kernels for the delta net recurrence. Q lora at short sequences fits twenty four gigabytes tightly; lora and D P O want one H one hundred; G R P O wants two, with prefix caching and the M T P head disabled in rollouts, because the hybrid state and the speculative head have had correctness issues in the gotchas chapter. For serving, quantize the projections with A W Q or F P eight and leave the recurrent state in sixteen bit, calibrate on long sequences, and verify the multi token prediction speculative config name for your V L L M version. The payoff is in the long prompt load test: a two hundred thousand token prompt costs far less K V memory than on a pure attention model, so its time to first token holds up where Qwen three four B at the same context would not.

The second interactive is the technique picker. Give it your goal, the amount of labeled data, a G P U budget and a model, and it writes the chain. A format goal with hundreds of examples is a lora fine tune and constrained decoding. A knowledge goal starts with retrieval, then continued pre training with replay only if you have a real corpus and an eight card node, or distillation from a larger model if one exists. A reasoning goal is rejection sampling fine tuning first, then G R P O. An agent goal is tool schemas and an evaluation harness before any training, then supervised fine tuning on successful trajectories, then multi turn reinforcement learning. A cost goal is quantization, distillation into a smaller student, and serving side wins like prefix caching and multi lora.

The third interactive is the V L L M command builder. Pick a model, the weight format, the G P U, tensor parallelism, context length and concurrency, and toggle multi lora, F P eight K V cache, and speculation. It emits the serve command with the right parsers for that model and a memory sanity check: weights plus overhead subtracted from ninety percent of the card, and the remainder divided by the per token K V cost to see how many tokens of cache you actually have against what the request mix can ask for. That check is where most out of memory errors in the gotchas chapter would have been caught before launch.

The chapter closes with a three week plan on an R T X fifty eighty with sixteen gigabytes. Week A is G P T O S S twenty B: serve it, measure time to first token and time per output token at each reasoning effort, then an attention only lora on a thousand harmony formatted examples and serve the adapter. Week B is Qwen three four B and the whole ladder: Q lora, merge, D P O, quantize two ways, serve, load test both thinking modes, and a small G R P O run. Week C is Qwen three point eight twenty seven B for inference only: four bit weights at long context, the needle and tool call regression tests, speculation on and off, and long prompt load tests to see the hybrid's advantage.
