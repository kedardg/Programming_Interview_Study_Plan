This is a short guided tour of the G P U mode lectures page, which brings the lectures repository into the notebook. The study plan's watch lists are built from these lectures, and until now the notebook only linked to them. Now the repository's supplementary material lives here: the code, the notebooks, and the slide decks up to ten megabytes, vendored under the Apache two point zero license, with the five largest decks linked back to GitHub.

The page lists all fifty-eight lectures in order. Each card gives the title, the speaker, the materials, and two tags. The first tag is the study plan step that assigns the lecture, so you can see at a glance that lecture one on profiling and integrating cooda kernels in PyTorch belongs to step seven, that lecture eight on the cooda performance checklist belongs to step eight, and that lecture twelve on flash attention belongs to step twelve. The second tag is the notebook chapter that teaches the same ground, so lecture twelve points at the attention chapter, lecture seventeen on collective communication points at the parallelism chapter, and lecture twenty-two on speculative decoding in V L L M points at the inference chapter.

The best way to use the page is read first, then watch, then work. Read the chapter the tag names, so the concepts are in place. Watch the lecture on the G P U mode channel. Then open the lecture's code or notebook from the card and run it yourself. Eight notebooks are rendered as pages you can read directly, with the outputs that were saved in them: Jeremy Howard's getting started with cooda from lecture three, Thomas Viehmann's compute and memory architecture notebook from lecture four, the going further with cooda notebook from lecture five, and the practitioner's guide to Triton from lecture fourteen, among others. The cooda source files from lectures eight, nine, and eleven, which cover the performance checklist, reductions, and sparsity, are the kernels the plan asks you to re-implement in step eighteen.

For the three-month schedule, the Tuesday lectures for the first eleven weeks are, in order, lectures one, two, five, nine, twenty, fourteen, the matmul worklog week, twenty-three, twelve, seven, and seventeen, and the cards for each are a click away from the schedule.
