Notes Library

Four open-source note collections vendored into this repo (verbatim Markdown under learn/vendor/notes/, rendered here in the notebook's theme) so the chapters' explanations can be followed by the practitioners' own working notes. They cover the bases the chapters summarize: Stas Bekman's training-infrastructure book, the SGLang/slime/verl developers' RL-system and inference internals, a full ML/GenAI system-design interview guide, and an AI-engineering Q&A bank. Each page links back to its source; licenses are preserved alongside the copies.

How to use: read the chapter first, then open the matching notes — Parallelism → ML Engineering's model-parallelism and network chapters; RL Systems and Frameworks → the verl/slime walkthroughs and weight-update notes; Inference → the SGLang KV-cache and scheduler notes; System Design → the ML system design worked designs; Customization & Evals → the fine-tuning and evaluation Q&A.
