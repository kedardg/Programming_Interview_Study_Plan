Behavioral Interviews

It is important to prepare stories from your past work experience for common behavioral and leadership questions ahead of time, using the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result). The Behavioral & Leadership Interview Prep Worksheet in Google Sheets includes many of the most popular questions, and working through it before your interviews is highly recommended. An Excel version is also available for offline use.

How to Answer Common Situational Interview Questions

Common Questions

  • Tell me about yourself.
  • What's your proudest project?
  • Why do you want to work here?
  • How do you manage projects under pressure?
  • How would you communicate technical challenges to non-technical stakeholders?
  • Tell me about a time you had a conflict with a team member.
  • Tell me about a time you made a mistake.
  • How do you stay up-to-date on advances in ML?
